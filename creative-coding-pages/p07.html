Project #7 AUTOBIOGRAPHICAL GAME

Discription:

My autobiographic game proposal is simple: to take care of my dog "OPPS". She is a important family member and good friend in my life, and my time with her has taught me patience, compassion and responsibility. Thus for my game I wanted to demonstrate the various challenges of dealing with pet care and show the joy of bonding with a furry companion. Main Character：my dog "OPPS" Beginning / Middle / End Scenes: washstation；drytable；kennel Mouse / Keyboard Interaction：click "next" go to next sence, press "s" save gif Assignment requirement link

Reflection:

In my coding process, I began the frist part by drawing the scene and gif of my dog. I worked on a low-resolution canvas draw the pixel, and later resized and repositioned the elements in Photoshop before uploading to p5js. I built the initial sence with preload(). Then I constructed the game's interactions using a combination of mousePressed and for Loops. For this part, is the most challenge that I sought guidance from my parents and followed some tutorials that employed if-else statements and true false to facilitate successful interactions between game tools and the puppy. Later scenes were created in similar ways, involving actions like dragging and dropping items to randomly generate water, fur, or hearts. The most significant challenge I encountered was make the gravity effect. I still struggl with understanding class() and variables, why does a set of arrays defined by a class need to be defined again and when go to draw() part we need to use variable?
